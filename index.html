Pick three. Hike. Run, throw, tackle, score.

On a phone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Then it opens like an app.

How to play

  • Stick Move your Pokémon
  • Hike Snap, throw, hand off, or tackle
  • Faces Pick a teammate (or switch defenders)
  • Ability Use that Pokémon’s special move
  • W A S D or Arrows Move
  • Space Snap · Throw · Handoff · Tackle
  • 1 2 3 Pick a receiver (or defender)
  • Shift Use Pokémon ability

Four downs to get a first down. Touchdowns are 7 points. No punts, field goals, or penalties in this first version.

How to play

  • You control one Pokémon at a time. On offense that is whoever has the ball.
  • The gold button (or Space) throws to the circled teammate, or hands off if they are next to you.
  • On defense, tap Tackle to lunge. Tap a face to switch who you control.
  • Get 10 yards in 4 downs, or score in the red end zone.

Final

You win!
