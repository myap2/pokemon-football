How to play

  • You control one Pokémon at a time. On offense that is whoever has the ball.
  • Space throws to the circled receiver, or hands off if a teammate is next to you.
  • On defense, Space lunges into a tackle. 1 / 2 / 3 switches who you control.
  • Get 10 yards in 4 downs, or score in the red end zone.

You win!
